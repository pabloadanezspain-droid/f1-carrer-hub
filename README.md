# F1 Career Mode Hub — foundation

A personal "living season" website for your F1 game career. Its visual language follows formula1.com. All of its content comes from your own career save.

## Pages (hash routes)
| Page | Route |
|---|---|
| Home: lead story, next race, latest result, standings podium + tables, news | `#/` |
| Calendar · race weekend | `#/schedule` · `#/schedule/<roundId>` |
| Race results index · race / sprint / qualifying result | `#/results` · `#/results/<roundId>/<session>` |
| Drivers' / Teams' standings (Wikipedia-style results matrix; `&edit=1` makes cells editable) | `#/standings/drivers` · `#/standings/teams` |
| Drivers directory · profile | `#/drivers` · `#/drivers/<id>` |
| Teams directory · profile | `#/teams` · `#/teams/<id>` |
| News index (category chips) · article | `#/news` · `#/news/<id>` |
| Insights: car development · title scenarios · season awards | `#/development` · `#/scenarios` · `#/awards` |
| Hall of Fame: champions, legends, records, drivers who left | `#/hall-of-fame` |
| Grid Showroom (one team at a time) · full-grid reveal | `#/showroom/<teamId>` · `#/showroom/grid` |
| Edit Career (incl. Portrait studio, Images) | `#/edit/<section>` |

Any season can be selected with `?season=<seasonId>`.

**The 2026 Real Grid (starter) season** is built by `tools/real-grid-2026.mjs` and has no results. It includes:
- 22 drivers and 11 teams, with colours and profiles
- the 23-round calendar
- the images attached to all of these

**Using it with your save:**
- If your browser already has a save, go to **Edit Career → Import / Export → Real 2026 grid** and click **Add to this career**. You can also start a new career from it there.
- Every image field in Edit Career has a picker listing the downloaded files. You can also paste any URL.
- Article images fall back to a trackside photo of a driver tagged in the story.

## Grid Showroom
- **Getting around:**
  - Each team gets a cinematic page with the team colours, a huge outlined team name, both drivers' portraits rising in, the car sliding in, and season stats.
  - A coloured wipe plays between teams, in the direction you're moving.
  - Navigate with the ‹ › buttons, the team strip, the arrow keys, or by swiping on a phone. Press **G** for the full grid.
- **Line-up shown:**
  - The line-up is the one for the season's next round, or the last round once the season is finished.
  - So after a transfer, the current season shows the new pairing, while older seasons keep showing their old pairings.
- **Motion setting:** the **Motion** button cycles between *auto* (follows your system's reduced-motion setting), *reduced* and *full*. It's remembered per browser.
- **Team card:** the **Team card** button (on the showroom and on every team profile) renders a 1080×1350 PNG to download.
  - Opened via the .bat, the card includes the real photos.
  - Opened as a plain file, it uses placeholders, because the browser blocks copying file photos into an image. A note tells you when this happens.

## Formula 2 and academies
- The starter career holds the real 2026 F2 grid next to the F1 grid: 22 drivers, 11 teams and 14 rounds, from fiaformula2.com. Faces, team logos, cars and news photos are in `assets/f2/` (list in `tools/f2-images.tsv`, data in `tools/real-grid-2026-f2.mjs`).
- **Edit Career → Career settings → Series in this career** picks Formula 1 only, Formula 2 only, or both. With both, the header shows an F1 / F2 switch and the F2 section lives under `#/f2/…` with a blue accent.
- **Academies** (Edit Career → Grid → Academies) link F2 drivers to an F1 team. Driver profiles show the academy badge, and F1 team pages list their juniors in their F2 suits. The starter links come from the badges on each driver's 2026 suit.
- Automatic news covers F2 rounds too ("the Baku Feature Race"). When an academy driver wins, reaches the podium or leads F2, an academy story appears with a photo of the parent F1 team. Every auto story gets a photo: the lead driver's photos first, then the season's photo pool.
- F2 faces have the same neck analysis as F1 faces, so a promoted junior gets an automatic draft in the F1 team's suit.

## Original 2026 calendar
The starter career uses the 24-race calendar planned before the war, as in the game (Bahrain and Saudi Arabia in April). Careers saved with the later 22-race calendar are switched automatically the first time they open (a backup is kept in the browser first). The switch moves every race to its original date and round number and adds the missing ones. Results stay attached to their race, line-up changes keep starting at the same race, and a backup is saved and downloaded first.

## Next season and stats
- **Edit Career → Seasons → Continue to the next season** (also offered on the home page when a season is complete) creates the next year for F1 and/or F2. It copies the calendar on next-year dates and the line-ups as at the last race. Nothing from the finished season changes, and a backup is saved and downloaded first.
- Driver ages are shown for the season you are viewing.
- **Standings → Stats** lists the season's stats and all-time stats across every season of the series (titles, wins, podiums, poles, points, best finish).

## Line-ups, transfers and free agents
- **Edit Career → Line-ups & transfers** opens a line-up board: every team of the season with 2 race seats and a reserve seat. Drag a driver onto a seat, or tap the driver and then the seat. Dropping on a taken seat swaps the two drivers. Dropping on **Free agents** releases the driver. Pick the round the change starts from; results already raced keep the old team.
- Drivers racing in the other series that year are listed under the board, so an F2 junior can be promoted with one drag.
- Released drivers appear as grey **Free agents** cards on the Drivers page.
- **Line-up** in the main menu (`#/lineup`, `#/f2/lineup`) shows a poster of the grid in team colours. "Save as image" downloads it as a 1600×900 PNG.

## F2 scoring and calendar
- Points follow the 2026 FIA F2 regulations: Feature race 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1, Sprint race 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1, 2 points for pole, 1 point for the fastest lap in each race (top 10 only).
- Baku has a second Feature race, as in 2026. The F2 calendar page is a Wikipedia-style table with sprint and feature dates and winners, and the F2 standings split each round into SPR and FEA columns.

## Portraits: faces × suits (Edit Career → Portrait studio / Images)
**A driver's face and a team's suit are separate sources.** For every driver-and-team pair, the site picks the portrait in this order:
1. **Approved composite** for that driver and team: your saved, versioned settings.
2. **Real portrait** taken in that team's suit. Library portraits are tagged with their team. Older seasons keep using these.
3. **Automatic draft (neck-fitted):** the driver's own face on the team's suit. The suit comes from an uploaded suit template, or else a team-mate's portrait from the neck down, or else a generated suit in the team colours.
4. **Clean placeholder** in the team colours, when there is no face at all.

### How neck fitting works (`src/neckfit.js`)
**Analysis.** Each portrait is analysed once. The analysis:
- finds the face and learns that driver's skin tone and beard colour;
- traces the real, curved collar line;
- measures the neck centre, the neck width and the collar tips;
- writes three derived files next to the original, which stays untouched:
  - **head mask:** head, hair, beard and his own neck;
  - **body mask:** the suit with all skin removed;
  - **face + extended neck:** his neck continued downward and outward from his own neck skin, blended so there's no hard edge under the beard.

**Composite.** Layered back to front:
1. His own head and neck.
2. His extended neck, visible only where the donor suit's old neck was, so skin can never show beside the collar.
3. The donor suit, skin-free, moved so its collar tips sit where his own collar was.
4. His real head again over the collar, so the chin and jaw are never covered.

His head is never stretched or moved; the suit adapts to his neck.

**Running the analysis:**
- Library portraits: run `node tools/analyze-portraits.mjs`. You can pass extra folders, e.g. `node tools/analyze-portraits.mjs assets/f2/drivers`. It writes `tools/portrait-fit.json` and `assets/f1com/masks/`, and needs Chrome.
- Uploads: analysed automatically in the browser. This needs a cut-out (transparent background). Other photos fall back to the simple cut-line and oval-mask mode.

**Checking results:** `node tools/qa-matrix.mjs` renders every driver in every team onto contact sheets in `reference/build-shots/qa-matrix-*.png`. For close-ups of specific pairs: `node tools/qa-matrix.mjs drv_x:team_y,drv_a:team_b`.

**Studio controls (neck-fitted mode):**
- Face position, scale and rotation.
- **Head over collar** line and its feather.
- Suit X, **Collar height** and suit scale.
- Suit tint and brightness.
- Colour match: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue and warmth.
- **Auto-fit neck & collar** recomputes everything.
- You can drag the preview to move the face, or scroll on it to scale.
- Previews show how it looks in a table circle, a driver card and the showroom.

**Approving and versions:**
- **Approve** saves a new version and supersedes the previous one; older versions stay in the history list.
- Use **Edit from this** to restore an old version, or **Withdraw** to go back to the automatic draft.
- The studio lists drivers who need review after a transfer.

**Originals are never modified:**
- Uploads are stored byte-for-byte in the browser's IndexedDB.
- A composite only stores references to its sources plus its settings, and it's layered live in the page. Nothing is baked into a new image.
- **Images** lists every upload and which composite versions use it. Files in use can't be deleted.
- **Export → Include uploaded images** puts the files into the JSON backup. On import, any missing files fall back to placeholders.

## Replacing art and branding
Edit `ASSET_MANIFEST` in `src/assets.js`. You can set the brand name or logo there, and map a team, driver, round or article id to an image path. You can also paste an image URL on the record in Edit Career.

Where nothing is set, generated SVGs are used:
- livery cars
- helmet portraits
- team badges
- flags

No F1 logos, team marks or photos are bundled. The Formula1 typeface comes from your own `Downloads/F1-Font-Family` folder and is embedded at build time. Titillium Web loads from Google Fonts, with a system-font fallback.

## Automatic news
After every saved result the site writes news for you (`src/newsgen.js`):
- **Facts:** winner, margin, pole, first win or podium of the season, win streaks, one-twos, retirements, leader trouble, championship lead changes, title clinched, home podiums, and your own team's result.
- **Stories:** every race gets a report; the two most newsworthy extra angles (title fight, milestone, drama, home hero, your team) get their own articles; the next race gets a preview.
- **Variety:** every headline and paragraph has several hand-written variants, picked by situation and a seed per race, so wording is stable but varied, and headline styles are not repeated within a season.
- **Your edits win:** an auto story you edit is never overwritten. Use **Rewrite** for new wording, or switch the feature off in Career settings.

## Newsroom
Every race weekend gets a timeline of stories, shown on the race page:
- Before the race: preview (Tuesday), press conference (Thursday), practice talking points (Friday), qualifying (Saturday).
- After the race: race report, team radio, team bosses, Winners and Losers, driver ratings, five things we learned, plus the best of the other angles.
- Between races: a long read on the hottest storyline when the gap is 12 days or more. Season review after the last race.

Story memory follows storylines across races: slumps and comebacks, podium runs, team-mate wars, title fights, rivalries, contract sagas and veterans. Career milestones (starts, wins, podiums, team droughts) and driver moods from the last three races shape headlines and quotes.

Storage: auto-written stories are rebuilt from results, so they are not saved. Only stories you edited, featured, rewrote or gave a picture are stored. Sync only rewrites seasons whose results changed, so long careers stay fast. Backups keep the last 3.

## Insights
- Car development: a pace index (0 to 100) per team per race, shown as a chart, plus a power ranking of the average gap per lap to the fastest car (qualifying lap times and race gaps, estimated from positions where times are missing). Big steps forward or back become news stories.
- Title scenarios article: published on the Wednesday before each race in the last third of the season while the title is open.
- Title scenarios: who can still win, the points left, and a table of what the leader needs at the next race to clinch. Shown on the standings and home pages and in race previews.
- Season awards: champion, Driver of the Year, Rookie, Overtaker, Qualifier, Mr Consistent, Comeback, Rivalry, Race of the Year, Team of the Year, Most Improved Car, Hard Luck. Published as an article after the last race.
- Hall of Fame: champions by year, legends, career records and drivers who left the grid. A "career in numbers" article runs when a driver with 20 or more starts leaves.
