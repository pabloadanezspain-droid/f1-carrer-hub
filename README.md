# F1 Career Mode Hub — foundation

A personal "living season" website for your F1 game career. Its visual language follows formula1.com. All of its content comes from your own career save.

## Open it
- **Recommended:** double-click **`Open Career Hub.bat`**. It starts a small local server with Python and opens http://127.0.0.1:5178. Team-card exports include the real photos only when the site is opened this way.
- **Easiest:** double-click `index.html`. It is a single self-contained file that works offline from `file://`.
- **Local server instead:** run `python -m http.server 5178` in this folder, then open http://127.0.0.1:5178.

On first run it loads a **fictional sample season**. A yellow banner marks it as invented. Use **Edit Career** to change it, or go to **Edit Career → Import / Export → Start empty career** to begin your own.

## Pages (hash routes)
| Page | Route |
|---|---|
| Home: lead story, next race, latest result, standings podium + tables, news | `#/` |
| Calendar · race weekend | `#/schedule` · `#/schedule/<roundId>` |
| Race results index · race / sprint / qualifying result | `#/results` · `#/results/<roundId>/<session>` |
| Drivers' / Teams' standings | `#/standings/drivers` · `#/standings/teams` |
| Drivers directory · profile | `#/drivers` · `#/drivers/<id>` |
| Teams directory · profile | `#/teams` · `#/teams/<id>` |
| News index (category chips) · article | `#/news` · `#/news/<id>` |
| Grid Showroom (one team at a time) · full-grid reveal | `#/showroom/<teamId>` · `#/showroom/grid` |
| Edit Career (incl. Portrait studio, Images) | `#/edit/<section>` |

Any season can be selected with `?season=<seasonId>`.

## Grid Showroom
- **Getting around:**
  - Each team gets a cinematic page with the team colours, a huge outlined team name, both drivers' portraits rising in, the car sliding in, and season stats.
  - A coloured wipe plays between teams, in the direction you're moving.
  - Navigate with the ‹ › buttons, the team strip, the arrow keys, or by swiping on a phone. Press **G** for the full grid.
- **Line-up shown:**
  - The line-up is the one for the season's next round, or the last round once the season is finished.
  - So after a transfer, the current season shows the new pairing, while older seasons keep showing their old pairings.
- **Motion setting:** the **Motion** button cycles between *auto* (follows your system's reduced-motion setting), *reduced* and *full*. It's remembered per browser.
- **Team card:** the **Team card** button (on the showroom and on every team profile) renders a 1080×1350 PNG to download.
  - Opened via the .bat, the card includes the real photos.
  - Opened as a plain file, it uses placeholders, because the browser blocks copying file photos into an image. A note tells you when this happens.

## Portraits: faces × suits (Edit Career → Portrait studio / Images)
**A driver's face and a team's suit are separate sources.** For every driver-and-team pair, the site picks the portrait in this order:
1. **Approved composite** for that driver and team: your saved, versioned settings.
2. **Real portrait** taken in that team's suit. Library portraits are tagged with their team. Older seasons keep using these.
3. **Automatic draft (neck-fitted):** the driver's own face on the team's suit. The suit comes from an uploaded suit template, or else a team-mate's portrait from the neck down, or else a generated suit in the team colours.
4. **Clean placeholder** in the team colours, when there is no face at all.

### How neck fitting works (`src/neckfit.js`)
**Analysis.** Each portrait is analysed once. The analysis:
- finds the face and learns that driver's skin tone and beard colour;
- traces the real, curved collar line;
- measures the neck centre, the neck width and the collar tips;
- writes three derived files next to the original, which stays untouched:
  - **head mask:** head, hair, beard and his own neck;
  - **body mask:** the suit with all skin removed;
  - **face + extended neck:** his neck continued downward and outward from his own neck skin, blended so there's no hard edge under the beard.

**Composite.** Layered back to front:
1. His own head and neck.
2. His extended neck, visible only where the donor suit's old neck was, so skin can never show beside the collar.
3. The donor suit, skin-free, moved so its collar tips sit where his own collar was.
4. His real head again over the collar, so the chin and jaw are never covered.

His head is never stretched or moved; the suit adapts to his neck.

**Running the analysis:**
- Library portraits: run `node tools/analyze-portraits.mjs`. You can pass extra folders, e.g. `node tools/analyze-portraits.mjs assets/f2/drivers`. It writes `tools/portrait-fit.json` and `assets/f1com/masks/`, and needs Chrome.
- Uploads: analysed automatically in the browser. This needs a cut-out (transparent background). Other photos fall back to the simple cut-line and oval-mask mode.

**Checking results:** `node tools/qa-matrix.mjs` renders every driver in every team onto contact sheets in `reference/build-shots/qa-matrix-*.png`. For close-ups of specific pairs: `node tools/qa-matrix.mjs drv_x:team_y,drv_a:team_b`.

**Studio controls (neck-fitted mode):**
- Face position, scale and rotation.
- **Head over collar** line and its feather.
- Suit X, **Collar height** and suit scale.
- Suit tint and brightness.
- Colour match: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue and warmth.
- **Auto-fit neck & collar** recomputes everything.
- You can drag the preview to move the face, or scroll on it to scale.
- Previews show how it looks in a table circle, a driver card and the showroom.

**Approving and versions:**
- **Approve** saves a new version and supersedes the previous one; older versions stay in the history list.
- Use **Edit from this** to restore an old version, or **Withdraw** to go back to the automatic draft.
- The studio lists drivers who need review after a transfer.

**Originals are never modified:**
- Uploads are stored byte-for-byte in the browser's IndexedDB.
- A composite only stores references to its sources plus its settings, and it's layered live in the page. Nothing is baked into a new image.
- **Images** lists every upload and which composite versions use it. Files in use can't be deleted.
- **Export → Include uploaded images** puts the files into the JSON backup. On import, any missing files fall back to placeholders.

## Replacing art and branding
Edit `ASSET_MANIFEST` in `src/assets.js`. You can set the brand name or logo there, and map a team, driver, round or article id to an image path. You can also paste an image URL on the record in Edit Career.

Where nothing is set, generated SVGs are used:
- livery cars
- helmet portraits
- team badges
- flags

No F1 logos, team marks or photos are bundled. The Formula1 typeface comes from your own `Downloads/F1-Font-Family` folder and is embedded at build time. Titillium Web loads from Google Fonts, with a system-font fallback.
